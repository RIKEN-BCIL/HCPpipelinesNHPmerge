# Non-Human Primate Neuroimaging and Neuroanatomy Project Collection

## Brain templates and atlases
 - This folder collects all the templates and atlases that are useful for resolving cortical folding, parcellation, and connectivity in various species
 - Currently available species are chimpanzee, rhesus macaque, cynomolgus macaque, and marmoset. Snow monkey and night monkey are also planned.
